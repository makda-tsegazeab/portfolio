Hello!

I am a Master's student in Science and Technology Policy at KAIST, with a background in Software Engineering from Mekelle University. My experience spans software development, machine learning, and teaching programming, including serving as a Teaching Assistant and later Head TA at AddisCoder.

My interests are now increasingly focused on AI governance and the societal impact of technology. I am particularly interested in how AI systems interact with institutions and communities, especially in low-resource and conflict-affected settings. I want to bring my technical background into technology policy and contribute to the development of AI systems and governance approaches that are more accountable, inclusive, and responsive to the people they affect.

I am now seeking to transition from undergraduate studies to a specialized master's program where I can deepen this inquiry. My goal is to gain the advanced technical and policy analysis skills necessary to contribute to more equitable and accountable technological development.
